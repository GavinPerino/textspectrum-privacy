Privacy Policy for TextSpectrum

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Gavin Perino ("we," "our," or "us") developed the TextSpectrum Android keyboard extension application. Because keyboard applications handle sensitive text input, we built TextSpectrum with strict offline isolation.

1. Zero Keystroke & Data Collection

TextSpectrum processes all keystrokes, characters, layout preferences, and color themes locally in temporary device memory. We do not collect, log, store, scan, or transmit anything you type, including passwords, messages, or personal information.

2. 100% Offline Architecture

TextSpectrum does not request or use Android internet permissions (android.permission.INTERNET). The application has no technical capability to connect to external servers, cloud databases, or analytics platforms.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Because no user data or usage analytics are collected, zero information is ever shared with or sold to third parties.

4. Children's Privacy

TextSpectrum does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
